Tips for Cracking Interviews

Here are some important tips to keep in mind during your interviews:

  1. Practice coding on platforms like LeetCode and HackerRank.
  2. Work on your communication skills to explain your thought process clearly.
  3. Review common interview questions and solutions beforehand.
  4. Maintain a calm and confident demeanor during the interview.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

  • Q: When do the placement drives start?
  • A: Placements usually begin in the final year of your course, typically in the 7th or 8th semester.
  • Q: How can I improve my chances of getting placed?
  • A: Focus on building your technical skills, communication abilities, and resume-building activities. Practice mock interviews and coding challenges regularly.
